Your messages stay on your phone

Nosy is a local-first SMS messaging and personal expense-tracking app. Here is the short version, then the full policy.

  • Not soldNo ads, analytics or accounts

Information Nosy accesses

When you choose Nosy as your device's default SMS app and grant the relevant Android permissions, Nosy can access and handle:

  • SMS and MMS messages, including sender/recipient information, message text, timestamps, attachments, and delivery status;
  • contacts, only if you grant the optional Contacts permission and use contact search or name display features;
  • notifications, only if you grant the optional notification permission; and
  • transaction and order information that Nosy derives on-device from messages, plus preferences such as parser corrections, opening balances, reminder choices, and app-lock settings.

Nosy uses this information to provide messaging, message search, local transaction categorisation, expense summaries, order tracking, notifications, and app-lock features. Parsed transactions may be inaccurate and should be reviewed; Nosy does not provide financial advice.

Storage and sharing

Nosy stores its message cache, derived transaction and order data, and settings locally on your device. The current version does not include cloud accounts, cloud synchronization, advertising, analytics SDKs, or an Internet permission.

Nosy does not sell your personal information. It does not transmit your SMS/MMS database or transaction database to our servers because the app has no such server connection.

If you explicitly share content, Nosy may pass the text you selected to the app you choose in Android's share sheet. That receiving app handles the data under its own privacy policy. Nosy does not otherwise share message or transaction content.

Nosy Pro and purchases

Nosy is free to use for the current month. Nosy Pro is an optional yearly subscription that unlocks older months, financial-year analytics and custom date ranges.

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. Nosy never sees or stores your payment details, name or email. Nosy asks the Google Play app on your device whether you hold an active subscription, and keeps only a local "Pro" flag and the time it was last confirmed. If Play cannot be reached, Nosy keeps Pro active for up to 14 days from the last confirmation. Nosy itself does not use the Internet. Manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play (Settings, then Manage subscription). Google's own privacy policy applies to purchases.

Without Pro, older months are hidden, not deleted. They reappear if you subscribe again.

Your choices and controls
  • You can decline optional Contacts and notification permissions.
  • You can remove Nosy as the default SMS app in Android Settings. Once it no longer holds that role, Nosy stops new SMS/MMS provider access and sending. Previously stored local data may remain visible until you delete it.
  • In Nosy Settings, Clear expense data removes locally derived transaction data. Delete local app data removes Nosy's saved local messages, transactions, settings, and temporary files, but keeps your Pro plan status so a subscription is not lost. Neither action deletes SMS or MMS held by Android's system messaging provider.
  • You can uninstall Nosy to remove its app data from your device. No account is required and there is no server-side account data for Nosy to delete.
  • App lock uses Android device authentication where available. It does not send biometric data to Nosy or its developer.
Device backup

Nosy's local database and preferences are excluded from Android backup and device-transfer rules. Your device manufacturer, operating system, carrier, and other apps may handle message data under their own policies.

Children

Nosy is not directed at children. Do not use the app where you are not legally permitted to use SMS messaging or personal-finance tools.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Continued use after the effective date is subject to the updated policy.
