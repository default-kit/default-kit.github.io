Tether · Effective 21 October 2026

Your commands stay on your network

Tether is an Android remote for your Mac. It pairs with the Default Kit companion app and controls volume, power, shortcuts and more over your local network. Here is the short version, then the full policy.

  • Your Mac onlyCommands go to the Mac you paired, not to us
  • Not soldNo ads, analytics or accounts
  • You can delete itForget Mac, or uninstall
  • You approve itEvery pairing is confirmed on your Mac
Information Tether accesses

Tether has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, messages or photos. To work, it handles:

  • the six-digit pairing code you type from your Mac's screen, your Mac's local address and device identifier, the phone name you send with your request, and the credential and certificate fingerprint your Mac returns when you approve. Tether does not ask for any permission beyond network access;
  • the commands you send, such as volume, brightness, power actions, app launches and key shortcuts, and the status your Mac reports back, such as volume level and the list of apps to show; and
  • app preferences, such as haptics, keep-screen-awake and your saved macros.

Tether uses this only to control your Mac. Power actions such as lock, sleep, restart and shut down ask you to confirm first.

Storage and sharing

Pairing details and preferences are stored on your phone. The pairing credential is encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. Tether does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not send data to the developer or any server we run.

Commands travel directly from your phone to your Mac over your local network. Pairing and every later command use your Mac's pinned certificate and are signed with a key held only by your paired phone. The first pairing trusts the certificate your phone sees at that moment, so pair on a network you trust.

Tether does not sell your personal information. If you use Nosy on the same phone, Tether can issue Nosy a one-time voucher to join your existing Mac pairing, and only when both apps are signed by Default Kit. The handoff stays on the device.

Your choices and controls
  • Pairing needs your approval on the Mac every time. Deny a request, or let the code expire, and nothing is paired.
  • Choose Forget Mac in the app to remove the stored credential and pairing from your phone. You can also remove the phone from Devices in Default Kit on your Mac.
  • You can uninstall Tether to remove all of its data from your phone. No account is required and there is no server-side account data for us to delete.
  • Your Mac's Default Kit app has its own privacy practices. This policy covers the Tether Android app only.
Device backup

Tether turns off Android backup, so its pairing details and preferences are not copied to Google Drive or to a new phone. You will need to pair again on a new device.

Children

Tether is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Continued use after the effective date is subject to the updated policy.
